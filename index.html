Planar Travel

Accessing other planes of existence is common through planar shrines which are powered through wish stones, however other methods of traversing between the planes are possible such as the infamous bag of holding-ception method.

Deities

The deities in this setting are those of the Dawn War Pantheon, The Sovereign Host and The Dark Six from Eberron. Deities from the Dawn war pantheon are limited to a physical form which is bound to their resident domain, but they still have divine influence across most planes of existence. The Sovereign Host and The Dark Six however are more omnipresent, closer to natural forces of nature than what is typically seen as a "god", not having physical forms.

The Material Plane

The material plane is the primary or "default" plane the campaign will take place in (Not necessesarirly the most frequent). The specific material plane this campaign will take place in is the same as the one in which the War Campaign took place in, but that is an event from the distant past and the Material Plane looks a bit different now. The species of the Material are far more interconnected, thanks not only to the shifting continents but also the efforts from Hope United and its adventurers.

(note: Material Plane refers to the Prime Material Plane, the cosmological plane of existance while material plane refers to an individual world within a crystal sphere within the infinite expanse of the Prime Material Plane)

The material plane will be a home base or HQ in a multiplanar war in which you will defend the your world from aberrations that have come from an unknown plane of existance. The material plane is split into two main continents: Vesiare and Yaberia. Vesiare is home to two nations, Nocklaan and Lasea. Nocklaan has somewhat unecessarily strict laws but allows great levels of freedom within those boundaries. The people there are fairly content and have good access to wishstone. Lasea however is far more strict, bordering on a full blown dictatorship. The people get by but have little access to wishstone beyond what is considered necessary.

Yaberia is home to only one nation, that being Antaria. Antaria is the closest nation to the ancient Hope United and has looser laws than Nocklaan but are just as harsh if not more so if those laws are broken. Citizens of Antaria have similar access to wishstone to those of Nocklaan, despite Nocklaan being richer in it than Antaria. Antaria is also where the campaign will begin.

Wishstones are rare gems formed through magic infusing into rocks and are located rarely on the material plane, but are far more common in the Feywild where magic is far more volatile. This has led to the Feywild being explored as a source of wishstone, although the unusual functions of the Feywild have made this all but impossible thus far. Wishstones are used most commonly as a source of magic for non-spellcasters, allowing for even a commoner to benefit from magical heat and light in their day to day life. They are also used in tandem with gears taken from Mechanus to automate simple things like a clock to complex elevator systems.

When wishstones use up teir magic, their effects are reversed to take in surrounding magic instead of providing it, leading to a magic nullification effect. Wishstones used for this purpose are called shadestone.

Rules

    Homebrew rules
  1. Aberrations are added to target lists for effacts and spells that target dragons, celestials, fey, fiends, monstrosities or oozes
  2. Several monstrosities have been changed to be aberrations
  3. Going a full day without a long rest inflicts 1 stack of exhaustion. This is reduced by 1 on a long rest as usual
  4. No sound based spell/ability can frighten unless the spell/ability states otherwise
  5. Taking the disengage action moves you 5ft (doesn't consume movement)
  6. If resting in an unfitting environment roll dc10 constitution saves until 3 successes or 3 fails
  7. Weakness system instead of vulnerabilities where damage for the type is increased by one die and/or triggers a special harmful effect
  8. Some enemies can inflict mind control on the players. The player will be given a new task to complete in the encounter however they wish
