Setting

The Multiverse

The universe of this setting utilises The Great Wheel cosmology, where the Prime Material Plane is placed within the Ethereal Plane whitch connects it to the inner planes which are the collective of all the elemental planes, the two energy planes, the Elemental Chaos, and the planes that formed between them. The Prime Material Plane is directly linked to its parallel planes the Fey Wild and The Shodowfell. The Astral Plane connects the Material Plane and all the inner planes to the outer planes. Accessing other planes of existence is common through planar shrines which are powered through wish stones, however other methods of traversing between the planes are possible such as the infamous bag of holding-ception method.

Deities

The deities in this setting are those of the Dawn War Pantheon, The Sovereign Host and The Dark Six from Eberron.

The Material Plane

The material plane is the primary or "default" plane the campaign will take place in (Not necessesarirly the most frequent). The specific material plane this campaign will take place in is the same as the one in which the War Campaign took place in, but that is an event from the distant past and the Material Plane looks a bit different now. The species of the Material are far more interconnected, thanks not only to the shifting continents but also the efforts from Hope United and its adventurers. (note: Material Plane refers to the Prime Material Plane, the cosmological plane of existance while material plane refers to an individual world within a crystal sphere within the infinite expanse of the Prime Material Plane) The Material Plane will be a home base or HQ in a multiplanar war in which you will defend the Material
